A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Everything to know before exploring the PureNest demo.

Is PureNest a real cleaning company?

No. PureNest Cleaning is a fictional company created to demonstrate a residential cleaning website. It does not operate a business or offer actual services.

What happens when I complete the quote form?

You will see a sample request summary in your browser. No price is calculated, no booking is made, and no information is sent to a server or saved by the form. The preview disappears when you reload the page.

How much would a cleaning cost?

The Instant Quote Calculator uses made-up demo prices to illustrate how an estimate could work. These are not U.S. market rates or real service quotes. For an actual cleaning, a real provider would need to confirm the home size, scope, condition, and additional requests.

Can I book a cleaning or contact the team?

No. There is no operating team, booking system, phone number, or email address behind this demo.

Are cleaning supplies included?

Supply arrangements are not offered or confirmed in this demo. In a real service, you would discuss products, surface care, sensitivities, and any special preferences directly with the provider.

Does this website use external resources?

No. The illustrations are original inline SVG artwork created for this demo. This site uses no external images, fonts, analytics, database, or third-party form service. Your demo selections stay in page memory only.
